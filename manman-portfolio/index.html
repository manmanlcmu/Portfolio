About

CHINA → JAPAN → U.S.

I grew up in China, spent 15 years in Japan, and now live in the U.S. Moving between three cultures gave me one habit I bring to every product: the same problem looks different depending on where you stand, so I try to stand in more than one place before deciding.

I had a stable career in Japan, and leaving it was not a small decision. But it also felt natural: the most exciting work in AI is happening in the U.S., and I want to build what comes next alongside the best people doing it.

I’m drawn to complex domains like factory floors, investment decisions, and research labs. I like breaking them down until the path from a fuzzy idea to something real becomes clear.
